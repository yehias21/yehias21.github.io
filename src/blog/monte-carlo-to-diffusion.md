# From Monte Carlo to Diffusion: Eighty Years of Learning to Sample

Every modern image generator is, at its core, answering a question that physicists at Los Alamos asked in the 1940s: *how do you draw a sample from a distribution you can describe but cannot directly sample from?* This post follows that single question across eighty years, from dart throwing on a quarter circle to models that turn pure noise into photographs.
